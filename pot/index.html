About

Data Engineer Focused On Reliable Analytics

I am a Junior Data Engineer and BS Computer Science student specializing in Data Science at the University of Engineering & Technology, Mardan. My work centers on building clean, validated, and analytics-ready datasets with Python, SQL, PySpark, Databricks, and Delta Lake.

I design ETL and ELT workflows that move data from raw ingestion into Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers, applying cleaning, transformation, validation, and quality checks so downstream teams can trust the results.

I enjoy connecting engineering discipline with business impact: data pipelines, Lakehouse architecture, data warehousing, and dashboards that help teams answer questions faster.
